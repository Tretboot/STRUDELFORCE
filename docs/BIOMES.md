# STRUDELFORCE MASTER - Biomes

## Overview
STRUDELFORCE MASTER features 8 unique biomes, each with its own theme, enemies, and boss.

### Chrome Tempest (ID: 0)
- **Description:** Mercury's industrial mining facilities, corrupted by the Devourers
- **Theme:** Industrial/Metal
- **Music Tempo:** 130 BPM
- **Enemy Count:** 15
- **Boss:** Mining Golem
- **Special Features:** Moving platforms, Lava pits, Corrupted machinery

### Great Bath (ID: 1)
- **Description:** Europa's underwater cities, home of the ancient Architects
- **Theme:** Water/Alien
- **Music Tempo:** 110 BPM
- **Enemy Count:** 12
- **Boss:** Leviathan
- **Special Features:** Underwater sections, Air pockets, Alien technology

### Mech Hive (ID: 2)
- **Description:** Devourer machine factory in the asteroid belt
- **Theme:** Mechanical/Horror
- **Music Tempo:** 140 BPM
- **Enemy Count:** 20
- **Boss:** Hive Queen
- **Special Features:** Moving assembly lines, Spawning enemies, Laser grids

### Void Reach (ID: 3)
- **Description:** Corrupted space where reality is twisted
- **Theme:** Cosmic/Psychological
- **Music Tempo:** 120 BPM
- **Enemy Count:** 10
- **Boss:** Void Walker
- **Special Features:** Gravity shifts, Teleportation, Reality distortions

### Core Furnace (ID: 4)
- **Description:** Devourer homeworld orbiting a black hole
- **Theme:** Epic/Final
- **Music Tempo:** 150 BPM
- **Enemy Count:** 25
- **Boss:** The Master
- **Special Features:** Black hole gravity, Multiple phases, Reality collapse

### Dessert Wastes (ID: 5)
- **Description:** Abandoned human colony on Mars
- **Theme:** Desert/Post-Apocalyptic
- **Music Tempo:** 125 BPM
- **Enemy Count:** 8
- **Boss:** Sand Worm
- **Special Features:** Sandstorms, Burrowing enemies, Hidden caves

### Forest Ruins (ID: 6)
- **Description:** Overgrown alien jungle on an unknown planet
- **Theme:** Nature/Alien
- **Music Tempo:** 115 BPM
- **Enemy Count:** 10
- **Boss:** Plant Guardian
- **Special Features:** Climbing vines, Poison spores, Camouflaged enemies

### Ice Caverns (ID: 7)
- **Description:** Frozen caves on a comet
- **Theme:** Ice/Stealth
- **Music Tempo:** 105 BPM
- **Enemy Count:** 6
- **Boss:** Ice Golem
- **Special Features:** Slippery surfaces, Ice bridges, Frozen enemies
